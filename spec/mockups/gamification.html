Motivation without pressure

Timely is an ambition cockpit, so the rewards point at progress toward the 1-year targets, not at ticks. A missed session never resets anything, nothing is sent to remind you to feel guilty, and every celebration is one line you can turn off.

Guardrails, applied to every mechanic above

  • No streak counter that resets to zero. Misses are shown as misses and cost nothing.
  • No guilt notifications. Reminders are off until the user turns them on, and never say what was missed.
  • Everything is opt-out, in Account: recaps, milestones and stages each have their own switch.
  • No leaderboard, no comparison with other users, no social feed.
  • Rewards follow ambition progress, not ticks. Ticking more does not raise a stage by itself.
  • Calm tone and calm motion: one line, a fade, no confetti, no sound, no red.
