The first plan, assisted

The first run is one short conversation: five questions, under three minutes, no AI. Fixed rules turn the answers into a draft plan (ambition, areas, goals, habits, a week and a first action today). The user edits the draft and accepts it. Nothing is saved before that, and Home is populated after Accept.

C. Rules that keep it honest

What the user always sees

  • The welcome says what the next three minutes do, and that nothing is saved before Accept.
  • "A draft from your answers. Edit anything." on the draft, and every field editable before accepting.
  • Nothing saved until Accept. Discard leaves no trace.
  • The first action is a real block for today, chosen from free time.

What the app never does

  • It does not guess numbers. Hours and times come from the answers and the presets, and the starter is always one tap away.
  • It does not add gamification, rewards or partner offers to the first run.
  • Sleep is set by default and is never a question.
  • It does not read the journal or any old data to build the plan.
